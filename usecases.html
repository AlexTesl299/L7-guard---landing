What you get

Security Without the Hassle

🌐Replace Your VPN Access

Say goodbye to resource-heavy desktop VPN clients and complex network settings. L7 Guard allows you to connect securely to your infrastructure right through a lightweight browser extension. It’s micro-segmentation made simple — granting access only to what’s needed, without exposing your entire network.

▶ Browser-Based Access

⏱️Time-Limited Access

Never worry about forgetting to revoke permissions again. Generate Just-In-Time (JIT) access tokens for freelancers or contractors that automatically expire when their shift is over. Give them exactly the time they need, and let the system lock the door behind them.

▶ Time-Limited Access

⏳The "One More Hour" Feature

Is a project taking a bit longer than expected? Instantly add extra time or bandwidth to an active session with a single click. There's no need to disconnect, generate a new token, or interrupt your contractor's workflow.

▶ Kill Switch

⚡Real-Time Magic

Experience the power of real-time synchronization. Whether you are changing permissions, revoking access, or updating limits, everything applies instantly across the board. No page refreshes or server restarts required — just seamless security management.

▶ WordPress Stealth Mode

👔Smart Role Management

Separate the heavy lifting from daily operations. Let your IT administrators handle the complex infrastructure and gateway setups, while giving project managers a simplified dashboard to quickly generate and hand out access tokens to their teams.

▶ Multi-Project Friendly

🏢Team Scaling

Growing your team shouldn't mean growing your IT headaches. L7 Guard’s architecture is designed to support multiple administrators and managers simultaneously, keeping your workflow smooth and secure no matter how large your operation gets.

▶ Private Demo Sites

🛡️External Audits

Make life easier for penetration testers and external review teams. Instead of creating individual accounts for every single person, issue one secure, shared access key for the entire team. Monitor their activity safely without compromising your core systems.

▶ WordPress Stealth Mode

🎛️Dynamic Permissions

Need to grant access to a new server or restrict an old one? Update allowed domains and routing rules instantly, even while the user is actively connected. Dynamic Access Control Lists (ACL) mean you are always in total command of who sees what.

▶ Multi-Project Friendly

🛑Stop Data Theft

Protect your valuable databases and intellectual property with strict bandwidth quotas. Set hard download limits for specific users. If someone tries to scrape your site or download more data than permitted, their connection is instantly severed.

▶ Private Demo Sites

👻Invisible WordPress

Your wp-admin page is a constant target for automated bots, scanners, and hackers. L7 Guard completely hides your login portal from the public internet. It’s simple math: if hackers can't see your login page, they can't attack it.

▶ WordPress Stealth Mode

🕵️Staging Protection

Protect your unreleased features and staging servers from the public eye. Easily isolate your development environments from search engine crawlers (like Google) and unauthorized visitors, sharing your work-in-progress only with the people who actually need to see it.

▶ Multi-Project Friendly

📑Multi-Tab Management

Manage several client projects effortlessly without constantly logging in and out. L7 Guard features Per-Tab Split Tunneling, allowing you to route different secure websites through completely different gateways—all at the same time, right from different tabs in your browser.
